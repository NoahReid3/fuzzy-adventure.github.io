A non-profit group working on development or environment, independent of government, NGO.
Aid given directly by one government to another, government or bilateral-style aid.
Aid from one country’s government to another’s, bilateral aid.
Aid channelled through international bodies (e.g. World Bank) for several countries, multilateral aid.
Rapid help after disaster (food, shelter, medicine), emergency aid.
Aid for years of projects in health, education, and infrastructure, long-term aid.
Rules or taxes that limit imports or exports, trade barrier.
A tax on imported goods, making them more expensive, tariff.
A limit on how much of a good may be imported, quota.
A policy to replace imports by producing goods at home, import substitution.
Growing the economy by selling more goods and services abroad, export-led growth.
A treaty between countries to reduce barriers to trade between them, free trade agreement.
Reducing dependence on one product or sector by growing new industries, economic diversification.
Using up resources faster than they can be replaced, resource depletion.
Using the environment in a way that keeps ecosystems healthy for the future, environmental sustainability.
Release of CO₂ and other greenhouse gases, often from burning fossil fuels, carbon emissions.
Building more wind, solar, and other renewable capacity, renewable energy development.
Rules and practices that balance use and conservation of soil, water, forests, and fisheries, sustainable resource management.
